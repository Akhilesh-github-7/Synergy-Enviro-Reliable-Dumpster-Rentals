FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01 What size dumpster do I need?

The right size depends on your project and the amount of waste. Our team can help you choose the best dumpster for your needs.

02 How long can I keep the dumpster?

Typically, rentals are for 7-14 days, but we offer flexible scheduling to accommodate your specific project timeline.

03 What materials can go in the dumpster?

Most non-hazardous construction debris, household junk, and yard waste are allowed. Hazardous materials, tires, and appliances may have restrictions.

04 Do you deliver outside Charlottetown?

Yes, we serve Charlottetown and surrounding communities across PEI. Contact us to verify delivery to your specific location.
